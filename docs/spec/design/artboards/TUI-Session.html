REQ-ADE-14, the say mode. One sentence at a time against the session already open. A phrase Yam can ground deterministically is grounded and run; a phrase it cannot stops and asks, and the three answers are the three groundings the product already has — a person points at it (REQ-REC-12), a model is asked where one is configured, or the line stays unbound and the flow says so. The flow accumulates in the middle pane and reaches disk only when you write it. Nothing here calls a model to execute: the model, when used at all, resolves a phrase to a binding once, and the finished flow replays without it.
